About Hatid

Hatid is a motorcycle ride booking website similar to Angkas or Grab. A user can enter a pickup location and a drop-off location, choose a ride type, see an estimated fare, and request a ride. The website will then simulate finding a nearby rider.

How it Works

  1. Enter your pickup and drop-off location.
  2. Choose a ride type (Standard, Priority, or Padala).
  3. Check the estimated fare, then press "Request a Ride".
  4. Wait for the system to match you with a rider.
  5. Your completed ride will be saved under Ride History.
